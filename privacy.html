Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Creator Upload ("the app") uses information. The app is developed by Atta Rao & M. Badar Rao ("the developers"). Contact: raobadar61@gmail.com.

In short: the app uploads the videos you choose to your own YouTube channel. Video analysis happens on your phone. The developers run no server and receive none of your videos, account details or upload history. Data goes only to Google: to YouTube for uploads, to Google sign-in, and limited diagnostic data from Google's ML Kit library (described below).

1. YouTube API Services

Creator Upload uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to your channel. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information the app accesses and why

2.1 Your Google account

When you tap "Connect Google account", Google's sign-in on your phone gives the app your Google account's basic profile (email address and name). The app shows the email address so you can see which account uploads go to. It is kept in the app's memory and is not written to the app's storage or sent to the developers.

The app then asks Google for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. Google returns a temporary access token that the app uses only to send your uploads to YouTube. Tokens are managed by Google Play services; the app requests them when needed, keeps them only in memory, sends them only to Google's YouTube API, and never logs or stores them.

2.2 Videos you choose

Videos are selected with Android's system picker. The app reads only the videos you pick. If Android provides a temporary copy, the app keeps that copy in its private storage while the upload waits in the queue and deletes it after the upload finishes or is cancelled. Your original video in the gallery is never changed or deleted. The app reads the video's file name, size, length and resolution.

2.3 On-device video analysis

To suggest a title, description, tags and thumbnail, the app extracts a few still frames (8 to 12) from the video and analyses them on your phone with Google's ML Kit (image labelling, text recognition and object detection) and the phone's own image functions (sharpness, colour, scene changes). The frames are temporary files in the app's private cache and are deleted after analysis, or when you upload or pick another video. The analysis results (for example "Car", text read on a badge, a colour) are used only to build the suggestions. Text read from your video is uploaded only if it becomes part of a title, description or tags that you upload.

2.4 Thumbnails

If you use a custom thumbnail, the app creates it on your phone from a frame of your video. It is uploaded to YouTube for that video. A small preview is kept in the app's upload history. If setting the thumbnail fails, the full image is kept for up to 7 days so you can retry, then deleted.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's features: signing in, analysing videos on your phone, preparing metadata and thumbnails, uploading to YouTube, and showing your queue and history. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone other than Google as described in section 3.

Creator Upload's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Cookies

The app does not use cookies or similar tracking. This website does not use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts.

7. Your choices and deleting data

  • Remove upload history: in the app, open History and remove an item or use "Clear history". This does not delete videos from YouTube.
  • Disconnect: in the app, Settings > "Disconnect and remove access" signs out and revokes the app's permission.
  • Revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete everything on the phone: uninstall the app, or use Android Settings > Apps > Creator Upload > Storage > Clear data.
  • Videos on YouTube are managed in YouTube Studio and follow YouTube's own policies.

8. Security

Data sent to Google uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Information kept by the app is stored in its private storage, which other apps cannot read. The app contains no passwords or secret keys.

9. Children

Creator Upload is a tool for people who manage a YouTube channel and requires a Google account; it is not directed at children. The "made for kids" choice in the app is YouTube's audience setting for a video, not information about the user.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data use changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed.
