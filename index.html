Creator Upload

A free Android app that uploads videos from your phone to your own YouTube channel. It can suggest a title, description, tags and a thumbnail by analysing the video on your phone, and keeps uploading in the background.

What the app does

Upload to YouTube

Pick a video, choose its visibility (Private by default) and whether it is made for kids, and upload it to your channel.

Auto Upload

One tap: the app analyses the video, writes the details, picks a thumbnail and uploads it using your saved settings.

On-device analysis

Frames are analysed on the phone to detect objects, readable text and colours. Your video and frames are not sent to the developers.

Background queue

Several videos upload one after another, keep going with the screen off, and resume after a lost connection.

Why the app asks for access to your Google account

To upload a video to your channel, the app must sign you in with Google and ask for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos"). It is used only to upload the videos you choose and to set their custom thumbnails, through the YouTube API Services. The app does not read your other videos, comments, subscriptions or channel data.

Creator Upload's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Free, no account with us

Creator Upload is free. It has no subscriptions, no ads and no server of its own: uploads go directly from your phone to YouTube. Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what data is used and where it goes.

Availability

Creator Upload runs on Android and is distributed directly by its developers as an APK file.

Creator Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Creator Upload is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube or Google.
